Chris Stapleton

Lexington, KY · Soul · Country · Blues · Americana

Chris Stapleton is one of the most celebrated voices in modern country and Americana music. His raw, soulful baritone and masterful guitar work have redefined what country music can be. From the platinum-selling debut Traveller (2015) to the sweeping Starting Over (2020), every record stands as a landmark of American roots music. A former staff songwriter in Nashville who co-wrote hits for dozens of artists before stepping into the spotlight.

Alan Jackson

Newnan, GA · Traditional Country · Honky-Tonk · Country Gospel

Alan Jackson is the undisputed king of traditional country music. Born in Newnan, Georgia, his authentic honky-tonk style, powerful baritone, and incredible knack for melody have made him one of the best-selling country artists of all time — over 80 million records sold worldwide. From dance-floor anthems to deeply moving gospel hymns, Alan Jackson's music is the heartbeat of American country. He was inducted into the Country Music Hall of Fame in 2017.

"I like to think that I'm just trying to be what I am, not trying to be what everybody wants me to be."

Alan Jackson — Gospel

Precious Memories · Hymns of the South · Country Gospel

Alan Jackson's Precious Memories (2006) and Precious Memories Vol. II (2013) are among the most beloved gospel recordings in country music history. Backed only by acoustic guitar and voice, these albums capture the raw, reverent beauty of classic American hymns. Jackson has said these are some of the most meaningful recordings of his career, a tribute to his Southern Baptist upbringing and his mother's love of gospel music.

"These songs have always been in my heart since I was a little boy sitting in church with my mama."
